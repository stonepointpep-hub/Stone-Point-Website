Information

Frequently asked questions

Where are testing records posted?

Each documented product has a testing-summary link on its product card. The summary lists the batch and available test categories without sending customers to a supplier-facing page.

Can I request documentation for a specific lot?

Yes. Contact Stone Point through the same channel used for your order and identify the product and batch shown in the testing summary.

Are these products intended for human use?

No. Products presented on this site are labeled for laboratory research use only and are not intended for human consumption or medical use.

What does a testing summary mean?

It identifies the test documentation listed for that batch. It does not reproduce numerical results or claim that Stone Point performed the analysis. Complete reports are available by request.
