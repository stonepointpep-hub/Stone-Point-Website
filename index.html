Information

Frequently asked questions

Where are testing records posted?

Testing records will be organized by product and lot in the COA section. The site can link directly to PDF or image files stored with the website.

Can I request documentation for a specific lot?

Yes. The final contact section can route documentation questions to your business email.

Are these products intended for human use?

No. Products presented on this site are labeled for laboratory research use only and are not intended for human consumption or medical use.

Can this site be updated automatically?

Yes. Once GitHub and Vercel are connected, changes pushed to the GitHub repository can automatically redeploy the live website.
